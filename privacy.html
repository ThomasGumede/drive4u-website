Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Who we are

Drive4U operates this website to provide information about e-hailing fleet management and to respond to enquiries. Contact us at info@drive4u.co.za.

2. Information we collect

When you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, enquiry category and message. Basic technical logs may also be processed by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

3. Why we use it

We use information to respond to enquiries, assess potential partnerships or driver applications, maintain records and protect our services. We process information in accordance with applicable South African privacy law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

4. Sharing and storage

We may use our hosting and email providers to process enquiries. We do not sell personal information. We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, subject to legal obligations.

5. Your rights

You may request access to or correction or deletion of personal information, where applicable, by contacting us. You may also raise a concern with South Africa's Information Regulator.

6. Cookies and external services

The website uses Google Fonts and links to WhatsApp. These third parties may process technical information when their services are accessed. We do not include advertising trackers by default.

7. Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices. Please review it periodically.

This is a starter privacy policy. Have it reviewed and updated to reflect your actual hosting, retention and operational practices before launch.
