Welcome to Drive4U

Ownership should not feel like a second full-time job.

We help vehicle owners access the earning potential of their cars while reducing the burden of managing drivers and day-to-day operations.

From driver placement and training to operational support and owner communication, Drive4U brings structure to your e-hailing journey.

What we do

Support for every stage of your e-hailing journey

Practical services that connect responsible drivers with vehicle owners and keep operations moving.

01

Driver Placement

We match eligible vehicles with suitable drivers from our prospective driver database.

02

Driver Training

Additional preparation promotes professional conduct, road safety and responsible vehicle care.

03

Fleet Operations

We support day-to-day vehicle and driver operations within the agreed management arrangement.

Vehicle Monitoring

Owner Communication

2
