Our Services

Practical e-hailing fleet management support for vehicle owners and drivers.

What we offer

Fleet management, without the everyday overwhelm

Services are delivered according to your signed management agreement and applicable platform and legal requirements.

01

Driver Placement

We match eligible vehicles with suitable drivers from our prospective driver database.

02

Driver Training

Additional preparation promotes professional conduct, road safety and responsible vehicle care.

03

Fleet Operations

We support day-to-day vehicle and driver operations within the agreed management arrangement.

04

Vehicle Monitoring

A Drive4U dashcam supports accountability and incident reviews alongside owner-provided tracking.

05

Owner Communication

We keep owners informed about their vehicles and agreed weekly or monthly payment processes.

06

Registration Support

We assist with relevant e-hailing platform registration processes and operational challenges.

For vehicle owners

Focus on your priorities. We support the operations.

Drive4U is designed for professionals, first-time investors, existing owners and South Africans living abroad.

  • Driver matching and placement from our database
  • Support with e-hailing platform registration
  • Operational assistance and vehicle-related communication
  • Dashcam installation for monitoring and incident review
  • Agreed weekly or monthly payment arrangements

For drivers

Start your journey with better preparation.

  • Driver interview and eligibility checks
  • Additional training before placement
  • Consideration for suitable vehicle opportunities
  • A driver community focused on teamwork and safety
