About Drive4U

Making e-hailing ownership easier, more manageable and better supported.

Our story

We connect vehicle ownership with operational support.

Drive4U is a South African e-hailing fleet management company committed to helping vehicle owners unlock the potential of their cars without the full daily burden of managing drivers and operations.

Finding trustworthy drivers, monitoring usage, handling operational issues and following up on payments can take significant time — particularly for owners with busy careers or those living abroad.

We maintain a prospective driver database, provide additional driver training, match suitable drivers to vehicles and support agreed operational responsibilities.
