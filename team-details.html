Driver Stories

Hear directly from people who have driven with Drive4U.

NM
Drive4U Driver · Over a year with the company

Ntuthuko Mdluli

“I have been driving with Drive4U for more than 12 months now. What I like most is that we are like a family. Drive4U drivers are there for one another, and we check up on each other to make sure everyone is safe while working.”

SM
From matric to e-hailing

Siphamandla Mbatha

“I joined Drive4U shortly after finishing matric. I did not have a job, and that is when I heard about the company. The training I received opened my eyes to many things about the e-hailing industry that I did not know before. By the time I hit the road, I felt prepared and confident to do the job professionally.”

LM
From looking for work to driving with Drive4U

Lungelo Makhathini

“Drive4U made it easier for me to get a vehicle and start working. Before I found the company, I was looking for people with cars who needed drivers, but I encountered scams and people demanding money in exchange for a job.”

“At Drive4U, I was invited for an interview, received training and was placed on the waiting list. Before long, I was allocated a good vehicle and started driving. I am still driving with the company and enjoying the experience.”
